Turkish Tavla Rules

Objective

Move all 15 checkers to your home board and bear them off.

Setup

  • Each player has 15 checkers
  • White: 2 on 24, 5 on 13, 3 on 8, 5 on 6
  • Black: 2 on 1, 5 on 12, 3 on 17, 5 on 19

Movement

  • White moves counter-clockwise (24→1)
  • Black moves clockwise (1→24)
  • Move checkers according to dice values
  • Doubles = 4 moves of that value
  • Must use both dice if possible

Key Rules

  • Hitting: Land on opponent's single checker (blot) to send it to bar
  • Blocked: Cannot land on point with 2+ opponent checkers
  • Bar: Must re-enter from bar before any other moves
  • Bar Entry: White enters on 19-24, Black on 1-6
  • Bearing Off: Only when ALL checkers in home board
  • Bear Off Rules: Exact die or higher if no checkers on higher points

Winning

  • First player to bear off all 15 checkers wins
  • No doubling cube in this version
  • Single game scoring only
